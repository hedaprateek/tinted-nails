How it works

Five steps. No lamp, no acetone.

Everything below comes in the box, including the 180/240 grit file and the wooden cuticle stick.

  1. Step 01

    Size

    Lay the tips beside each nail and pick the width that touches both sidewalls. Note the numbers — the next set is instant.

    2 min
  2. Step 02

    Prep

    Push cuticles back with the wooden stick, buff the shine off, then wipe each nail with the alcohol pad.

    3 min
  3. Step 03

    Stick

    Adhesive tabs for a two-day wear, or a thin bead of glue across the plate for the full two weeks.

    1 min
  4. Step 04

    Press

    Set the tip at the cuticle line, roll it down, and hold for thirty seconds per nail. Pressure is what makes it last.

    5 min
  5. Step 05

    Finish

    File the free edge to the length you actually want, and skip water for the first hour.

    2 min

Wear & care

Treat them like a good manicure, not like a sticker.

Glue-on sets hold for one to two weeks. Tab sets come off the same evening. Either way, the tips survive removal — most people get three or four wears out of a set before the gloss gives up.

How do I take them off?
Soak in warm soapy water for ten minutes, then ease a cuticle stick under the edge. Never pry — that's your own nail plate coming with it.
Will they fit small nails?
Twelve widths cover 6 mm to 17 mm. If you're between sizes, take the smaller one and file the sidewalls.
Can I shower and cook in them?
Yes, after the first hour. Wear gloves for washing up — hot water is what loosens glue first.
What if a nail pops off?
Clean off the old glue with the file, re-glue, hold thirty seconds. Spare glue is in every box.
